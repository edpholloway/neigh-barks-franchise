Our story

Built from a real need.

Neigh-Barks was created around the everyday challenges of caring for animals and finding the products they need close to home.

Our beginnings

Rooted in Beaver County.

Becky and Ed Holloway founded Neigh-Barks in 2017 after experiencing how difficult it could be to find affordable animal feed and the right supplies without travelling between stores.

They wanted to create a welcoming, practical destination serving the many kinds of animals people care for, from household pets to horses and poultry.

What began as a local idea continues to shape Neigh-Barks' approach to service, retail and community.

Our perspective

Every animal matters.

Customers rarely fit into a single category. Our stores serve the variety of animals that are part of people's lives.
