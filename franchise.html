Common questions

What prospective owners ask.

Do I need experience in animal retail?

Experience with animals, retail or business management can certainly be an advantage, but we're equally interested in your enthusiasm, work ethic and commitment to excellent customer service. Our franchise team will discuss the experience, qualifications and qualities that contribute to successful Neigh-Barks ownership.

Are territories available?

Yes, franchise territories are available for consideration in select communities. Territory boundaries and availability are evaluated based on local demographics, market potential and the needs of the community. Our franchise team will work with you to explore opportunities in your preferred area.

What investment should I plan for?

The minimum initial investment is currently estimated at $194,000, including the franchise fee and anticipated start-up costs. The total investment will depend on factors such as location, store size and individual business requirements. We'll provide detailed financial information and explain the investment structure as you explore the opportunity.

What is the first step?

Reach out to our franchise team and tell us a little about yourself and the community you're considering. We'll arrange an introductory discussion and explain the next steps.
